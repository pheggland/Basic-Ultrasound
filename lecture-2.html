Module 2: Ultrasound Physics

Understanding the Physics of Ultrasound

In this module, you will learn about the fundamental physics principles that make ultrasound imaging possible, including sound waves, frequency, wavelength, and propagation.

Learning objectives in this lecture:

  1. Explain the properties of sound waves (frequency, wavelength, amplitude)
  2. Describe how ultrasound waves propagate through different tissues
  3. Understand the relationship between frequency and image resolution
  4. Explain acoustic impedance and its role in image formation
